Edge comparison on black and light backgrounds

  • Changed: partial-alpha boundary colours and their matte are unmixed from the original white image, guided by the aligned black reference. No blanket edge fading or smoothing is applied.
  • Protected: every original opaque pixel and the interior remain bit-identical, retaining the three blades, sharp metallic details, brushed highlights and bronze shadows. No new foreground is introduced outside the supplied shield’s support. The exact existing lettering image and its placement are reused.
  • Checked: the result recomposited on white matches the original white-reference appearance to within one 8-bit value per channel. PNGs have real RGBA alpha, transparent corners and no embedded white or checkerboard canvas.
  • Limit: the source’s opaque light rim/grain is deliberately preserved rather than guessed away or painted over. The boundary alpha is a raster estimate, not recovered authored alpha. A clean original vector/export is needed to certify every edge pixel; this proof must not be called a guaranteed exact master.
  • Unchanged: uploaded originals, prior proof assets, live header, products, clothing, packaging, favicon and other branding. Nothing has been published. Approval here concerns the candidate asset only, not live replacement.
