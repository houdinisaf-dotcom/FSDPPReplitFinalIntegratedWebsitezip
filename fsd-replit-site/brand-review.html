Source and preparation notes

  • Neither original has transparency: the black image is a 1154 × 713 RGB JPEG; the white image is an 827 × 542 RGB PNG.
  • The larger black reference supplies the metallic pixels. The white reference was inspected to check the shield outline, blade details, spacing and letterforms.
  • A soft alpha matte removes the black canvas and unpremultiplies edge pixels. This is estimated raster transparency, not the original authored alpha. Some edge/shadow behaviour on light backgrounds remains source-limited.
  • No AI generation, tracing, vector reconstruction, recolouring of opaque metallic artwork, aspect-ratio distortion or upscaling was used.
  • The full raster is 934 × 604; the 520 × 132 header export supports up to 260 × 66 CSS pixels at 2× density. Lossless WebP alternatives accompany PNG masters. Larger print or unlimited-scale use needs the existing original vector or transparent master.
  • All proposed files are isolated in the review directory. The live header, favicon, uploaded originals and every clothing, hardware, packaging and product design remain untouched.
