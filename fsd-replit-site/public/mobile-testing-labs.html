01 / The Testing Framework

The initiative assesses how mobile testing units could support apparel and equipment trials, training-experience demonstrations, and structured feedback closer to practitioners.

Events, gyms, and universities are potential settings, not confirmed venues or partners. The approved scope allows anonymised performance and fit insights with participant consent, subject to a privacy notice and appropriate safeguarding controls.
