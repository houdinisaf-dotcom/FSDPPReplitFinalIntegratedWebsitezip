Initiative Framework & Rollout

This initiative is developing mobile testing units to bring FSD apparel, equipment, and training experiences closer to practitioners, while gathering feedback to guide future development.

  • Potential testing settings: Events, gyms, and universities are prospective settings, not confirmed venues or partners.
  • On-site product testing: The framework includes hands-on product and training-experience trials; no current trial is represented as active.
  • Participant feedback: The source scope calls for anonymised performance and fit insights with participant consent. No data-collection activity is represented as live.

Status: Operating or rollout underway. No mobile testing unit, venue, test, or data-collection activity is currently represented as operational.
