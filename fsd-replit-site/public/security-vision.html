01 / The Strategic Vision

As FSD evolves from a movement-led performance house into a broader operational ecosystem, its strategic vision includes professional security provision. Drawing on the principles of self-discipline, tactical readiness, and physical capability, FSD aims to develop services for selected events, venues, and private functions across the United Kingdom.

The proposed framework recognises that security involves more than physical presence. It includes emotional intelligence, risk mitigation, de-escalation, and adherence to applicable regulatory frameworks.

02 / SIA Licensing & UK Legislation

Any future security provision would be developed around applicable legal and regulatory requirements:

  • SIA Licensing: Deployed personnel would hold the applicable Security Industry Authority (SIA) licence for their role, including Door Supervision or Close Protection where required by the Private Security Industry Act 2001.
  • Vetting: The proposed framework includes background checks, British Standard (BS 7858) screening, and ongoing professional development.
  • Health, Safety & Licensing: Planned controls include local-authority licensing conditions, counter-terrorism awareness and Martyn’s Law readiness, and emergency evacuation procedures.

03 / Proposed Capability Areas

Event & Crowd Management

Proposed scope includes ingress and egress control, perimeter security, pit management, and public-safety planning for events, exhibitions, and brand activations.

Venue & Hospitality Protection

Proposed scope includes front-of-house security and door supervision for venues seeking consistent guest-safety and service standards.

Specialized Close Protection

Potential future scope includes tailored details for VIPs, executives, and delegations, subject to relevant licensing, risk assessment, and legal requirements.

Development status: Strategic expansion plan. Not an active operational tender. Deployment and commercial contracting are subject to corporate structuring, formal SIA Approved Contractor status where applicable, and appropriate insurance and legal-compliance frameworks.
