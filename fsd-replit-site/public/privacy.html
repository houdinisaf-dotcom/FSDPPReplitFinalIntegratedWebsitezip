Privacy

Pre-launch website. FSD is in development and is not yet trading or taking orders. This wording is subject to legal review before sales begin.

Who is responsible?

FSD (Fitness & Self Defence) is a brand in development, operating initially from Manchester, United Kingdom. A registered business name and address will be published here once registration is complete. Contact: info@ / sales@ FSD business email (being set up; the full address will appear here once it is live).

What we collect today

  • If you join the pre-launch waitlist: the email address you enter, the consent wording you agreed to and the time you agreed.
  • Nothing else is collected through forms. There is no account sign-up, and no payment details are taken on this website.

Why we use it

Waitlist emails are used only to tell you about the FSD launch and related updates you asked for. The lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Waitlist records are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until the pre-launch list is closed, whichever comes first.

When FSD starts trading

When online sales open, FSD plans to use Stripe to process card payments. Card details would be entered with Stripe and would not be stored by FSD. Order details (name, delivery address, email and order history) would then be needed to fulfil orders. This notice will be updated, and you will be told, before any of that begins.

Sharing and your rights

Waitlist data is stored in the website's hosting database and is not sold or shared for marketing. Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting info@ / sales@ FSD business email (being set up; the full address will appear here once it is live). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
