Cookies

Pre-launch website. FSD is in development and is not yet trading or taking orders. This wording is subject to legal review before sales begin.

Summary

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or third-party analytics. It uses a small amount of browser storage so the site works as you would expect.

What is stored in your browser

  • Bag (local storage): items you add to the preview bag, so they are still there when you return.
  • Saved items (local storage, fsd-product-wishlist-v1): products you save.
  • Theme (local storage, fsd-theme): your light or dark display choice.
  • Animation state (session storage): remembers which collection reveals you have already seen, cleared when you close the tab.

Your choices

This storage is needed for the features above and stays on your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. If FSD adds analytics or payment features later, this page will be updated first, and optional cookies will only be set with your consent.
