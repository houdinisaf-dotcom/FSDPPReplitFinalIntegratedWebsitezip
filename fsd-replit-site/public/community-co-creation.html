This operating initiative is developing consent-based ways for the community to inform future work. No workshops, product trials, or submissions are open.

01 / Co-Creation Framework

Community Co-Creation is an operating initiative developing structured ways for customers, athletes, instructors, and practitioners to inform future FSD products, training experiences, and initiatives. Its framework includes structured feedback, workshops, and collaborative testing as potential formats.

Any future participation would require clear consent, privacy, safety, moderation, and content-use rules. Feedback does not guarantee that an idea will be adopted. Participation is feedback only; it does not grant shares, tokens, profit-sharing, or voting rights over the company.
