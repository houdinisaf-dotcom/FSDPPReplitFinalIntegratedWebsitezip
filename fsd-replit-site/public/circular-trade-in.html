Circular Trade-In Programme

Wear. Return. Reuse. A more circular approach.

Initiative Framework & Rollout

The Circular Trade-In Programme is developing a framework to extend the useful life of FSD apparel and equipment through potential inspection, repair, resale, and recycling pathways. Its aims are to reduce textile waste and support more circular product use.

Status: Operating or rollout underway. This page does not currently accept trade-in requests or specify eligibility, credit, collection, or processing terms.
