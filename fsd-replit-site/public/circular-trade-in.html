Circular Trade-In Programme

Wear. Return. Reuse. A more circular approach.

This proposed concept assesses potential product-reuse pathways. No trade-ins, collection, or resale service is available.

01 / Circularity Framework

The Circular Trade-In Programme is developing a framework to extend the useful life of FSD apparel and equipment through potential inspection, repair, resale, and recycling pathways. These are proposed routes to assess, not a service currently offered.

Potential environmental and commercial benefits remain objectives to measure. No waste reduction, resale outcome, or cost efficiency has been verified.

02 / Potential Programme Pathways

Inspection & Grading

Define how product condition, safety, and hygiene could be assessed; no inspection or grading service is operating.

Repair & Refurbishment

Assess whether suitable products can be repaired or refurbished to documented standards; no repair offer or process is available.

Reuse & Resale

Explore whether eligible products could be safely reused or resold; no pre-loved stock, resale channel, or eligibility policy is live.

Recycling & Responsible Routing

Assess appropriate end-of-life routes with qualified providers; no recycling partner, destination, or zero-waste outcome is confirmed.

03 / Potential Value to Assess

Longer product use
More accessible reuse options
Customer retention
Operating efficiencies
Environmental impact to measure

These are potential aims, not verified outcomes or guarantees.

04 / Conditions Before Any Launch

  • Define accepted products, condition criteria, and eligibility.
  • Select suitable partners and collection infrastructure.
  • Set inspection, repair, safety, hygiene, and resale standards.
  • Define credit, funding, accounting, legal, and consumer terms.
  • Validate unit economics and measure environmental impacts before making claims.

Status: Strategic concept; no trade-in service is available. This page does not currently accept trade-in requests or specify eligibility, credit, collection, or processing terms. No partner or operating process is confirmed. No zero-waste or verified environmental-impact claim is made.
