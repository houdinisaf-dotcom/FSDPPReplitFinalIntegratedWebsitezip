Local Micro-Manufacturing

On-demand production. Closer to our communities.

This active initiative assesses local, small-batch production. No manufacturing partner, hub, production site, or service is live.

01 / The Production Framework

The initiative is assessing whether a local, small-batch production framework could support FSD apparel and selected equipment, subject to quality, safety, cost, and commercial feasibility.

Potential partners and locations remain unconfirmed. Custom fit, design variations, and community- or event-specific ranges are feasibility questions, not current customer offerings.

Potential aims to assess include shorter lead times, lower transport emissions, reduced material waste, and selected customisation; none are measured or promised.

02 / Production Capabilities

Small-Batch Production

Assess whether smaller production runs closer to customers can meet FSD quality, safety, cost, and delivery requirements; no production is underway.

Selected Customisation

Potential fit or design variations may be evaluated for feasibility; custom orders and event-specific ranges are not available.

Quality & Performance

Any future process would need documented material, safety, and performance checks; no local process or test result is confirmed.

Local Partnerships

Potential manufacturing and skills partners may be assessed; no partner, location, or programme is confirmed.

03 / Potential Strategic Value

More responsive inventory
Selected customisation
Local skills and partner opportunities
Material waste and transport impact to measure

These are aims for assessment, not verified outcomes.

04 / Progression Requirements

  • Identify and qualify suitable manufacturing partners and locations.
  • Validate production cost, capacity, quality, safety, and performance.
  • Complete legal, regulatory, and intellectual-property review.
  • Measure environmental and operational impacts before making claims.
  • Confirm funding and risk controls before any production pilot.

Status: Operating or rollout underway. No local manufacturing hub, production site, or service is currently represented as operational. No manufacturing partner or custom-order service is confirmed. No delivery-time, material-waste, or emissions reduction is measured or promised.
