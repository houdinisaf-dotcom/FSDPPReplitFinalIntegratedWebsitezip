03 / MARKET Market and Customer

Targeting distinct customer cohorts who value durability, technical utility, and clean minimalist design over loud branding.

  • Performance-focused lifter: Movement, durability, secure storage, premium appearance.
  • Combat-sport / self-defence participant: Mobility, breathability, toughness, community identity.
  • Style-led active consumer: Gym-to-street versatility.
  • Modest-performance customer: Coverage, opacity, comfort, requires wearer co-design.
  • Gym, academy or corporate buyer: Consistent co-branded supply (after validation).

05 / OPERATIONS Supply Chain & Fulfilment

Rigorous operational oversight designed to protect cash flow and eliminate early inventory waste.

  • Landed-cost and contribution analysis before any price or order approval.
  • Receiving quarantine, SKUs/HEK validation, return triage, and incident procedure.
  • Pilot fulfilment from own stock, transitioning to approved 3PL providers when volume dictates.

08 / ROADMAP Roadmap & Gates

Twelve weeks to turn key assumptions into hard operational evidence.

  • Weeks 1–2: Freeze brief, palette, bi-folds; trade-mark screening.
  • Weeks 2–4: Swatches, quotes, samples; initial testing.
  • Weeks 4–6: Fit, mobility, opacity, wash and durability testing.
  • Weeks 6–8: Provisional supplier, written terms, waitlist.
  • Weeks 8–12: 50–100 unit pilot; track sales, returns, CAC, AOV.
