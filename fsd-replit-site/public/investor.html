01 / PROPOSITIONPre-Commercial

The Foundation

FSD is a proposed premium activewear brand informed by gym training, functional fitness, and self-defence culture. Pre-commercial with no verified trading history or secured investment.

  • Initial pre-seed: Up to £75,000 proposed.
  • First products: AEROFORGE Training Tee & Hybrid Short.
  • Direct-to-consumer first, expanding to B2B tests.
02 / PRODUCTAEROFORGE Range

Product Architecture

Designed around movement-led performance apparel combining technical utility and restrained luxury.

  • Training Tee (FSD-AF-T01): £35–£45 (Sample development)
  • Hybrid Short (FSD-AF-S01): £40–£50 (Sample development)
  • Tech Set (Hoodie + Jogger): £100–£140 (Conditional on pilot)
03 / MARKETAudience & Validation

Market & Customer

Targeting distinct customer cohorts who value durability, technical utility, and clean minimalist design over loud branding.

  • Performance-focused lifters and combat-sport participants.
  • Style-led active consumers and gym-to-street buyers.
  • Validation via 20–30 structured customer interviews and waitlist pilots.
04 / REVENUEArchitecture

Revenue Streams

Diversified revenue streams structured across staged growth horizons, beginning with core product D2C sales.

  • Core products: Direct sales and repeat purchases.
  • Business accounts: Gym and academy contract supply.
  • Community services: Courses, coaching, events.
  • Brand & IP partnerships: Licensing and operator fees.
05 / OPERATIONSSupply Chain

Supply Chain & Fulfilment

Rigorous operational oversight designed to protect cash flow and eliminate early inventory waste.

  • Landed-cost and contribution analysis before order approval.
  • Receiving quarantine, SKU/HEK validation, and return triage.
  • Pilot fulfilment from own stock, transitioning to approved 3PL.

  • Total Pathway: £175,000 – £225,000

Roadmap & Gates

The first twelve weeks are designed to turn key assumptions into hard operational evidence.
