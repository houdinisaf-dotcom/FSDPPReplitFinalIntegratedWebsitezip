The Proposition

A premium technical-activewear brand informed by gym training, functional fitness and self-defence culture. Apparel is the entry point; a wider ecosystem is built only where evidence supports it.

Investment case

  • Distinct position between premium performance apparel, self-defence culture and technical-athletic design.
  • Focused launch range to validate demand before broad inventory commitments.
  • Capital released against evidence: approved samples, measured demand, contribution margin.
  • Multiple later routes: e-commerce, studios, B2B uniforms, selective wholesale, events, licensing.
  • Long-term ecosystem across apparel, education, community and digital content.

Operations and Supply Chain

  • Dual-track sourcing: technical blanks with local branding for early tests, plus custom AEROFORGE samples developed in parallel.
  • Supplier leads (Decisive Wear, Minku Apparel, Sports Creative, Ocean Prints, Leon’s Fabric Superstore) are prospective only and unverified.
  • Matched requests for quotation by MOQ, lead time, payment milestones, delivery terms (FOB or DDP) and inspection rights.
  • Landed-cost and contribution analysis before any price or order approval.
  • Receiving quarantine, SKU-level stock control, returns triage and incident procedure.
  • Pilot fulfilment from own space; third-party provider only at volume.

Legal readiness (UK)

  • Consumer sales: pre-contract information and cancellation rights reviewed before taking orders; no blanket “no returns”.

  • Trade marks cleared before launch; no mark described as registered until granted.
  • Insurance (product, public, cyber, events) assessed by a broker.
