01 / Research Framework

The initiative assesses whether independently validated research could ever inform general, non-personalised training or nutrition education. No individual interpretation, diagnosis, treatment, or health recommendation is in confirmed scope.

Any future work involving genetic or microbiome information would require strict privacy protections and appropriate clinical oversight. No genetic or microbiome data is currently collected or analysed.

02 / Research & Governance Pillars

Evidence & Validation

Review whether reliable scientific evidence exists for any proposed application; no findings or validated method are available.

Clinical & Research Governance

Any future study would require appropriately qualified independent partners, ethics review, and relevant regulatory approvals; no partner is confirmed.

Data Privacy & Stewardship

Any future research would require informed consent, data minimisation, security, and safeguarding controls. No genetic or microbiome data is collected or analysed.

Appropriate Scope

This is not a testing, diagnostic, treatment, or personalised health service. No individual training or nutrition guidance is offered.
