What was implemented

  • Additive public entry paths for existing products, men's/women's/accessories collections, all seven clothing lines, story and public divisions. Each opens the existing product dialog, filtered catalogue or established banner rather than a replacement storefront.
  • Shared build-time and browser metadata, including standalone public pages. A verified origin will generate absolute sharing images, canonical links, Open Graph URLs and a public-route sitemap.
  • Real branded 404 responses, source-map request denial, immutable caching for hashed build assets, and a stable React vendor chunk. No dependencies were added.
  • A native-dialog lifecycle guard prevents a queued old close event from clearing a newly reopened product or moving focus away from it.
  • Preservation and regression testing of seven collection banners, scroll-active highlights, approved far-right logos, restrained motion, variant-safe galleries, full-screen photography, zoom/panning and cart controls.
  • No missing photographs were replaced with unrelated images. No fabricated photographic angles, models, 3D viewer or single-image “360°” simulation was added.

Available recording review

Two older FSD screen recordings were found in the project: approximately 49.93 seconds and 4.47 seconds, both 2560 × 1600 H.264. Neither contains an audio stream, so there were no spoken instructions to transcribe. Neither filename explicitly identifies it as the requested WhatsApp reference.

Review used representative extracted frames at approximately five-second intervals for the longer recording and one-second intervals for the shorter recording. It was a visual sampling review, not an audio review or a frame-by-frame certification of animation timing.

  • The longer recording shows an older FSD hero, sticky header, scroll-through editorial sections, Universe cards, a catalogue, visual image archive, VIP section, and a collection overlay with category chips and product previews.
  • The shorter recording shows an older multi-column Collections overlay inside Replit's preview.
  • The current site already supplies the corresponding navigation, editorial hierarchy, collection filtering and product handoffs. These were tested rather than replaced with the recorded older UI.
  • The historical visual archive and VIP controls were not restored against later scope decisions. A white catalogue panel in an older recording was not assumed to be a current defect. No recorded artwork, legacy logo or unverified product mapping was imported.
  • Recommendation: retain the current unified black/gold navigation and approval boundaries. Use an explicitly identified WhatsApp reference, if different, for any further motion-specific comparison.
