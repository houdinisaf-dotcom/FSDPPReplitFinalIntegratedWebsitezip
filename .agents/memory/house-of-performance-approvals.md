---
name: House of Performance design approvals
description: Brand direction and staged approval boundaries for the FSD website refinement
---

FSD’s website direction is “THE HOUSE OF PERFORMANCE,” with the philosophy “Luxury in its expression. Performance in its purpose.” Preserve the campaign statement “BUILT FOR THE DRIVE WITHIN.”

**Why:** The user wants a distinctive luxury performance house spanning apparel, modest combat, technology concepts, equipment and future flagship experiences, not a generic fitness retailer.

**How to apply:** Use the existing FSD photography and approved shield-led asset in isolated proposals. Do not generate substitute product imagery, redesign branding, rename products, overwrite originals or rebuild the site from scratch.

The user requires an audit, homepage/mobile visual proofs, both a minimal translucent dropdown and a floating editorial panel, and interactive collection-banner demonstrations before major implementation. Category selection must reveal subcategories without scrolling; navigation requires a deliberate destination choice.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires approval of both visual direction and navigation behaviour before site-wide changes.

**How to apply:** Keep proposals separate from the current site until the user approves implementation. Prototype destination views are not evidence of existing production routes. Keep Women’s Modest Combat prominent and provide its dedicated landing destination when implementing the approved design. Do not publish or enable checkout.
