---
name: Missing imagery policy
description: Preserve all FSD products and sections when final photography is unavailable.
---

Never remove, hide, or skip an existing product, collection, banner, or hero because
its final image is unavailable. Use named, premium black/charcoal placeholders with
the current approved accent and preserve its dimensions and working controls.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the complete experience available while adding
final images gradually, rather than an image-dependent selection of content. The
current Phase 2 brief confirms gold accents and explicitly rejects lime.

**How to apply:** Keep missing colour/size/variant images distinct where relevant.
Use organized image mappings for later replacement; do not substitute unrelated stock
photos or collage boards. Follow the most recent explicit palette direction for each
phase rather than carrying an earlier placeholder accent forward.
