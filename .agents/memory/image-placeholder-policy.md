---
name: Missing imagery policy
description: Preserve all FSD products and sections when final photography is unavailable.
---

Never remove, hide, or skip an existing product, collection, banner, or hero because
its final image is unavailable. For SHOP product cards and product-detail galleries,
use FSD-branded light-neutral placeholders with a clear product/view label and IMAGE
PENDING status; do not use near-black panels for these missing product-media slots.
Preserve the media dimensions and working controls.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the complete experience available while adding
final images gradually, rather than an image-dependent selection of content.
The user also specified a light-neutral media canvas for missing product views, so
the former black/charcoal default is not appropriate for SHOP cards or PDP galleries.
Approved photo mappings and the active visual palette can change by phase.

**How to apply:** Keep missing colour/size/variant images distinct where relevant.
Use organized image mappings for later replacement; do not substitute unrelated stock
photos or collage boards. Test missing-image fallbacks with an explicitly unmapped
variant or simulated missing asset, and wait for the image load/fallback state before
asserting; do not expect a currently mapped colourway to use a placeholder. Use
front/back/detail labels in PDP galleries and PRODUCT IMAGE / IMAGE PENDING in cards.
For non-product hero or section media, follow that section's latest explicit palette
direction rather than carrying the product-media treatment across the whole site.
