---
name: FSD change scope
description: Standing boundary for future implementation on the FSD website.
---

For future site changes, inspect first, reuse existing systems, and implement only the explicitly requested feature. Do not redesign, duplicate, or refactor completed sections, or alter approved products, prices, variants, branding, navigation, galleries, search, bag, or checkout unless explicitly authorized or essential to the requested feature.

**Why:** The owner issued a direct development-continuity instruction to avoid unrelated work and rework.

**How to apply:** Keep edits local, explain any essential cross-cutting changes, and report what changed and why. Respect priority-specific review gates before moving to the next phase.
