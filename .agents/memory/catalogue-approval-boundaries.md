name: Catalogue approval boundaries
description: How to interpret product decisions and unresolved manufacturer details
---

Product names, prices and construction briefs approved by the user remain pre-launch development decisions, not evidence of completed manufacture or verified performance.

**Why:** The user approved complete commercial dumbbell sets spanning 10kg–50kg without specifying increments or quantities, and explicitly left barbell contents undecided. Those omissions must not be filled with assumed industry-standard configurations.

**How to apply:** Keep missing manufacturer details explicit, distinguish proposed construction from verified materials, and obtain factual specifications before assigning package contents. The user confirmed all prior holds remain and the site stays pre-launch with no checkout.

Publication holds apply to reference galleries as well as product listings and roadmaps.

**Why:** A concept can otherwise remain publicly promoted through a named design-library image after its catalogue record is held.

**How to apply:** Check all public surfaces when changing hold status; retain source files for internal review without rendering held references.

The reviewed Performance/Cover hijab and HYDRATE, MOTION, NEXUS, SONIC and PULSE
step-counter image candidates were not approved for product mappings.

**Why:** The user selected “Do not add these matches” when shown the candidate
sheet, without providing a reason. Visual similarity or a descriptive asset name
must not be treated as approval.

**How to apply:** Leave these products unmapped unless the user gives a new
explicit approval. Consult `handoff/catalog/image-review.md` for the reviewed
references; do not interpret the decision as an instruction to remove the
separate design library.

VIP sign-up collection is deferred until the destination and approved privacy/consent wording are ready.

**Why:** The user chose to defer collection rather than use a provisional or nonfunctional sign-up form.

**How to apply:** Keep VIP access informational and explicitly unavailable for registration until those details are supplied; never display a false submission-success state.

The Aegis gilet’s supplied composite and its individual crops are visual references only. The master-record Black / Gold colour and £69.99 price are not approval to sell; rear construction and product specifications remain unverified.

**Why:** The user approved only a private visual/layout proof, asked to keep master values unchanged, and explicitly withheld commercial approval.

**How to apply:** Keep Aegis imagery in private review surfaces, mark rear construction and technical details as pending, and do not enable checkout or promote the catalogue values as sale terms without separate approval.

Avoid the exact visible phrase “in development” across the public site.

**Why:** The user asked for all instances of that wording to be removed.

**How to apply:** Use honest pre-launch or proposed-concept wording instead, without implying that a product is available to buy.
