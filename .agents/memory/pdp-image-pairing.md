---
name: PDP image pairing
description: Product detail galleries pair approved, variant-matched images while preserving their original composition.
---

Pair a secondary product image only when an approved image for the selected colourway exists; otherwise keep a single primary image. Preserve each photograph’s source proportions rather than cropping it into a fixed portrait frame.

**Why:** FSD imagery mixes landscape lifestyle photographs and portrait detail views. Arbitrary cropping can remove garment or brand details, while mismatched colourways can misrepresent a product.

**How to apply:** When adapting the shared product detail gallery, map secondary images to the selected colourway, retain the image’s natural aspect ratio, and preserve the single-image fallback.
