---
name: PDP image pairing
description: Product detail galleries pair approved, variant-matched images while preserving their original composition.
---

When approved front and rear views exist for the exact selected colourway, use them as the desktop product-page pair. Preserve an explicitly approved listing-hero order, such as Velocity’s, and keep all other mapped views available in the gallery. If a matching pair is absent, retain the existing primary image or named placeholder. Preserve each photograph’s source proportions rather than cropping it into a fixed portrait frame.

**Why:** Front/rear pairing supports the approved editorial product-page direction, while an explicitly curated hero can be part of a product’s approved presentation. FSD imagery mixes landscape lifestyle photographs and portrait detail views; arbitrary cropping or mismatched colourways can misrepresent a product.

**How to apply:** Match both views to the exact selected colourway, retain natural aspect ratios and the single-image fallback, and do not replace an explicitly approved hero order.
