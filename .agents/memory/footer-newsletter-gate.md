---
name: Footer newsletter gate
description: Delivery and consent boundary for the FSD footer newsletter control
---

Keep the footer newsletter email field and JOIN control disabled until a delivery provider and destination, plus approved consent, privacy, and retention details, are configured. Do not imply a signup is sent or stored.

**Why:** The footer has no configured email delivery path; a disabled, non-submitting control avoids collecting data or claiming an outcome that cannot be fulfilled.

**How to apply:** When enabling this footer control, establish opt-in language and handling, wire a verified delivery destination, and test successful and failed submissions before presenting JOIN as active.
