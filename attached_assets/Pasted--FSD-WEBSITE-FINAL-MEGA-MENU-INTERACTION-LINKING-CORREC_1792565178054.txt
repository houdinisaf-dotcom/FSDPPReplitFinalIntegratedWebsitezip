## AMENDMENT 2 — MAKE COLLECTION AND SUBCATEGORY LINKS WORK

### Middle column

Hovering or focusing on a subcategory should update the relevant featured content.

Clicking a subcategory should navigate to its existing collection or product page.

### Right column

The existing featured collection button must open the correct destination for the currently selected category or subcategory.
