# FSD WEBSITE — PHASE 2
## Cinematic Product Storytelling & Premium E-Commerce Interactions

### PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing FSD Fitness & Self Defence website with sophisticated product storytelling, cinematic transitions, responsive product interactions and an elevated luxury e-commerce experience.

This is Phase 2 of the FSD Digital Experience enhancement programme.

**IMPORTANT: Do not rebuild, redesign or replace the existing website.**

Phase 1 introduced the shared motion system, subtle ambient lighting, navigation transitions and foundational microinteractions. Reuse those systems rather than introducing competing animations or unnecessary dependencies.

The objective is to make browsing FSD products feel immersive, elegant, fluid and technically sophisticated.

---

## 1. PRODUCT CARD ENHANCEMENTS

Enhance existing product cards across all FSD collections.

### Desktop interactions

On hover:

- Introduce subtle product image magnification, approximately 1.025–1.045x.
- Add a soft champagne-gold ambient backlight behind the product image, where visually appropriate.
- Apply a gentle upward translation of approximately 2–4 pixels.
- Reveal existing product actions with a restrained fade or slide.
- Transition borders and shadows smoothly without creating bright gold outlines.
- Maintain the original product image aspect ratio.

Where genuine secondary product images already exist, support a smooth transition to the secondary image.

Never substitute an unrelated image or automatically generate alternative product views.

### Mobile interactions

- Do not rely on hover.
- Use clear, accessible product actions.
- Preserve natural touch scrolling.
- Avoid unnecessary animated overlays.
- Keep product cards fast and responsive.

All product cards must remain visually consistent with the existing FSD design.

---

## 2. CINEMATIC PRODUCT DETAIL PAGES

Enhance the existing individual product pages without changing their core layouts unnecessarily.

### Product imagery

Implement:

- Smooth transitions between gallery images.
- Elegant thumbnail selection states.
- Subtle crossfade when switching product photographs.
- Responsive image zoom where useful.
- A premium full-screen image viewer with an unobtrusive dark backdrop.
- Keyboard and touch navigation where applicable.

Image transitions should feel smooth, immediate and deliberate.

Do not introduce exaggerated 3D rotations, spinning product images or artificial CGI effects.

### Product information

Improve the visual hierarchy of:

- Product title.
- Collection name.
- Product description.
- Price.
- Colour and size options.
- Product features.
- Technical specifications.
- Availability indicators.
- Purchase-related controls.

Use the existing FSD typography, spacing and colour palette.

### Animated information panels

Where existing product pages have expandable content, enhance the sections for:

- Product technology.
- Materials and construction.
- Fit and sizing.
- Care instructions.
- Delivery information.

Use smooth accordion transitions with clear open and closed states.

Avoid animation that delays access to information.

---

## 3. IMMERSIVE PRODUCT STORYTELLING

Introduce reusable editorial storytelling sections for selected flagship products.

These sections should combine premium imagery with concise, meaningful product information.

### Example: FSD Aeroforge Women's Performance Top

Use existing approved product photography to highlight:

- Sculpted athletic fit.
- Technical stretch construction.
- Breathable mesh ventilation.
- Raglan shoulder design.
- Flatlock stitching.
- Thumbhole cuffs.
- Signature FSD shield detailing.

Create a cinematic product story using large photography, concise headings and restrained scroll-based reveals.

Suggested storytelling sequence:

**ENGINEERED TO MOVE**

Introduce the garment with a striking full-width photograph.

**PRECISION IN EVERY DETAIL**

Reveal technical construction and material features.

**PERFORMANCE WITHOUT COMPROMISE**

Highlight comfort, fit and intended athletic applications.

**THE FSD STANDARD**

Conclude with the product's brand positioning and an existing product-page CTA.

Only display product features that are confirmed in the catalogue or supplied specifications.

Do not invent material compositions, performance certifications or technical claims.

### Reusable design

Build this storytelling system as reusable components so it can later support:

- FSD Aeroforge.
- FSD Velocity Performance Sports Hijab.
- FSD Signature Performance Sports Bag.
- FSD performance accessories.
- Other approved flagship products.

Do not automatically apply lengthy storytelling sections to every product.

---

## 4. INTERACTIVE PRODUCT FEATURE HOTSPOTS

Where suitable, introduce subtle interactive hotspots on selected product photographs.

### Interaction design

- Use small, restrained champagne-coloured markers.
- Reveal a compact information panel on click or keyboard activation.
- Explain a specific product feature.
- Allow the panel to close naturally.
- Keep the original image visible and unobstructed.

For example, the Aeroforge top could feature hotspots for its mesh ventilation, thumbhole cuffs and chest badge.

Hotspots must be positioned accurately on the relevant product image.

Do not invent feature locations or specifications.

### Mobile behaviour

Use tap activation with accessible dismissal.

Avoid covering important product details.

Do not enable hotspots on every image by default.

---

## 5. COLLECTION-TO-PRODUCT TRANSITIONS

Improve transitions when visitors move from collection listings into individual product pages.

Desired experience:

- Product selection should feel immediate and connected.
- Use subtle content fades and image reveals.
- Preserve correct browser navigation.
- Support browser back and forward behaviour.
- Restore collection browsing position where appropriate.
- Avoid blank screens, heavy loading animations or disruptive full-screen transitions.

If the current application architecture supports shared-element transitions safely, consider them for selected product images.

Otherwise, use lightweight route transitions that preserve reliability.

Do not add complex routing libraries solely for visual effects.

---

## 6. PREMIUM COLOUR AND SIZE SELECTORS

Enhance existing product option selectors.

### Colour options

- Smooth selection indicators.
- Clear active and inactive states.
- Subtle champagne focus treatment.
- Correct product imagery for each available colour.
- No artificial colour options.

### Size options

- Refined selection transitions.
- Accessible keyboard interaction.
- Clear unavailable-size indicators.
- Existing size-guide access where available.
- Appropriate selection feedback.

Never change product availability, inventory, prices or variant data.

---

## 7. PREMIUM SHOPPING INTERACTIONS

Prepare refined interaction states for the existing shopping interface.

Where purchase functionality is already authorised and operational, enhance its presentation without altering the underlying commerce logic.

Potential enhancements include:

- Subtle confirmation animations.
- Smooth mini-cart panel transitions.
- Refined quantity controls.
- Clear loading and success states.
- Accessible error messaging.

**CRITICAL: Preserve existing checkout restrictions.**

If checkout or purchasing is currently disabled, keep it disabled.

Do not enable payment processing, place orders, activate checkout or introduce a live purchasing workflow.

Do not display fake order confirmations.

---

## 8. FSD SIGNATURE AMBIENT LIGHTING

Extend the Phase 1 lighting system selectively into product storytelling.

Use:

- Soft champagne-gold edge illumination.
- Subtle image backlighting.
- Restrained shadows.
- Dark graphite surfaces.
- Low-intensity hover highlights.

The effect should resemble precision architectural lighting.

Avoid:

- Neon gold borders.
- Excessive glow.
- Constantly pulsing elements.
- Bright gradient backgrounds.
- Heavy glassmorphism.
- Distracting particle effects.

The product photography must remain the focal point.

---

## 9. EDITORIAL SCROLL EXPERIENCES

For selected collection landing pages, introduce subtle cinematic scroll reveals.

Possible treatments:

- Heading fade and upward reveal.
- Product photography appearing with controlled opacity.
- Gentle background depth changes.
- Staggered product-feature introductions.
- Smooth section transitions.

Avoid excessive scroll-jacking or forced scroll snapping.

Do not prevent users from scrolling normally.

Content must remain readable and accessible with animations disabled.

---

## 10. PERFORMANCE AND ACCESSIBILITY

All enhancements must be engineered for performance.

Requirements:

- Reuse Phase 1 motion tokens.
- Prefer GPU-friendly transform and opacity animations.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript animation loops.
- Lazy-load noncritical media.
- Prevent cumulative layout shifts.
- Maintain responsive image sizing.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Preserve visible focus indicators.
- Ensure adequate contrast.
- Keep mobile interactions responsive.
- Test on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Do not introduce large animation libraries unless the existing stack genuinely requires them.

Prioritise smooth interaction over visual complexity.

---

## 11. PRESERVATION RULES

Do not:

- Rebuild the existing website.
- Replace the FSD master logo.
- Alter product branding.
- Delete product photographs.
- Rename collections.
- Modify product descriptions without approval.
- Change prices or inventory.
- Break existing category filters.
- Replace floating navigation panels.
- Introduce unnecessary new pages.
- Enable checkout or payment processing.
- Publish the website without approval.
- Remove or override Phase 1 enhancements.

All work must integrate with the current FSD website architecture.

## 12. IMPLEMENTATION WORKFLOW

### Step 1 — Audit

Inspect the current codebase and identify:

- Existing product-card components.
- Product-detail page components.
- Image-gallery implementation.
- Product variant selectors.
- Collection page templates.
- Routing architecture.
- Phase 1 animation utilities.
- Existing shopping controls.

Explain which components can be enhanced safely.

### Step 2 — Implement core product interactions

Prioritise:

1. Product-card hover and focus effects.
2. Product-gallery transitions.
3. Product-detail information animations.
4. Colour and size selector refinements.

### Step 3 — Build reusable storytelling components

Create the editorial storytelling system and demonstrate it on one existing flagship product using approved imagery.

### Step 4 — Add optional interactive features

Implement product hotspots and enhanced collection-to-product transitions only where they improve usability.

### Step 5 — Test

Verify:

- Desktop responsiveness.
- Mobile performance.
- Navigation and routing.
- Product image accuracy.
- Product data integrity.
- Keyboard accessibility.
- Reduced-motion behaviour.
- Existing checkout restrictions.

### Step 6 — Report

Provide a concise implementation report covering:

- Components modified.
- New reusable components created.
- Dependencies introduced, if any.
- Performance implications.
- Functionality preserved.
- Any features requiring approval.

## FINAL DESIGN STANDARD

The completed experience should feel like a fusion of:

**Apple:** precision, fluidity and interaction quality.

**Nike:** athletic energy and immersive product storytelling.

**Luxury fashion houses:** restraint, exclusivity and editorial presentation.

**FSD:** strength, discipline, confidence and performance.

Every animation must have a purpose.

Every interaction should make the product easier to understand or the website more enjoyable to use.

**The final result must feel expensive, deliberate and effortless — never overanimated or gimmicky.**
