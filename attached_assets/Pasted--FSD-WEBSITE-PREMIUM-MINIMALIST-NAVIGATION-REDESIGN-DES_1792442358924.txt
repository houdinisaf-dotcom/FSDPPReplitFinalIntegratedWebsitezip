# FSD WEBSITE — PREMIUM MINIMALIST NAVIGATION REDESIGN

## DESIGN DIRECTION

Refine the existing FSD website navigation and collection discovery experience.

Take inspiration from the design principles of Apple and Nike:

- Apple: clarity, restraint, precision, subtle transitions and exceptional usability.
- Nike: confident editorial photography, strong visual hierarchy and effortless product discovery.
- FSD: distinctive black, graphite and satin champagne-gold identity.

Do not copy either company's website directly.

The result should feel minimalist, elegant, sophisticated, premium and contemporary.

Avoid anything overly futuristic, cluttered, flashy or gimmicky.

## PRIMARY ISSUE TO FIX

The current website relies too heavily on scrolling between homepage sections.

Visitors need a simple way to discover subcategories directly from the header and main collection banners.

Clicking a collection should reveal its subcategories in place before navigating away.

## NAVIGATION EXPERIENCE

Create a refined navigation system with subtle floating or expanding menus.

The exact presentation may be selected based on what works best with the existing website.

Possible treatments include:

- A translucent dropdown beneath the header.
- A softly expanding floating navigation panel.
- A minimal full-width category reveal.
- A compact mobile navigation sheet.

Do not force a large circular bubble or oversized glass modal.

Prioritise simplicity, elegance and usability.

## VISUAL DESIGN

- Deep-black and graphite backgrounds.
- Restrained champagne-gold accents.
- Elegant typography and generous whitespace.
- Subtle transparency and background blur where useful.
- Minimal borders and shadows.
- No excessive glow, neon, animation or decorative effects.
- No unnecessary icons.

The navigation should feel integrated into the website rather than sitting awkwardly over it.

## INTERACTION RULES

On desktop:

- Clicking a primary category reveals its submenu.
- The menu opens smoothly without scrolling the page.
- Visitors can select a subcategory or close the menu.
- Navigation occurs only when a destination is selected.

On mobile:

- Use a clean expanding menu or navigation sheet.
- Ensure all subcategories are accessible.
- Avoid tiny buttons and hover-dependent controls.

Use animations of approximately 180–280 milliseconds.

Respect reduced-motion settings.

## HOMEPAGE BANNERS

Keep the existing FSD hero and collection imagery.

Refine the collection banners so they feel like premium editorial entry points.

Use short headings, generous spacing and minimal calls to action.

Clicking a main collection banner should open the relevant category selector rather than simply scrolling further down the homepage.

## PROTECT EXISTING ASSETS

Do not alter:

- FSD logo artwork.
- Product imagery.
- Apparel designs.
- Equipment designs.
- Brand colours.
- Product names.
- Existing catalogue records.

Do not enable checkout or publish the website.

## IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

First review the current header, collection cards and click behaviour.

Create two small navigation prototypes using the existing website styling:

Option 1: Minimal translucent dropdown.

Option 2: Elegant floating category panel.

Show desktop and mobile previews of both.

Recommend the option that best supports FSD's premium positioning and usability.

Do not implement a full redesign until I approve the preferred interaction.

The goal is to make the website feel as refined and intuitive as a premium global sportswear and technology brand, while remaining unmistakably FSD.
