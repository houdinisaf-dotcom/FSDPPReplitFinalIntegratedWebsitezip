# REPLIT PROMPT — FSD MODEST FLOW INTERACTIVE COLOUR SELECTOR

## OBJECTIVE

Implement the approved interactive product-selection experience for **FSD Modest Flow — Women's Modest Performance Activewear Set**, allowing customers to explore all six approved colour editions through a sophisticated, luxury-style floating product panel.

**COLLECTION PLACEMENT: Apparel → Women's → Modest Activewear**

The interaction must match the existing FSD Fitness & Self Defence website aesthetic: matte black, graphite, subtle champagne-gold accents, elegant typography and smooth, understated animations.

**IMPORTANT: This is a targeted product interaction enhancement, not a website redesign.**

Preserve the existing website, approved interactive mega-menu, navigation, product catalogue, imagery, layouts and Phase 2–4 improvements.

Do not publish or activate checkout.

---

## 1. PRODUCT DISCOVERY EXPERIENCE

Add **FSD Modest Flow** to the existing **Modest Activewear** collection within the Women's Apparel section.

The product consists of the approved coordinated modest activewear outfit:

- Extended-length performance tunic/overshirt.
- Long sleeves with thumbhole cuffs.
- Concealed side-zip openings.
- Relaxed wide-leg performance trousers.
- Coordinated performance hijab as shown in the approved imagery.
- Small matte champagne-gold Serene Monogram details on the wrist cuff and trouser thigh area.

**Use the approved product imagery. Do not redesign the outfit or replace its logo.**

Display **one approved campaign hero image** for this product.

Use the separate hero photograph featuring the model in the Deep Burgundy Modest Flow outfit in the alternative warm architectural gym setting, with the large window and softer editorial background.

This campaign hero is a fixed promotional image.

**Do not change the hero photograph when a customer selects another colour.**

When the customer clicks the FSD Modest Flow product, open a premium floating product-selection panel rather than redirecting them unnecessarily.

The panel should feel like an extension of the website's existing full-width interactive mega-menu.

## 2. FLOATING PRODUCT-SELECTION PANEL

Create a refined, responsive overlay with:

### Left section

- One large product image.
- Clean presentation against a charcoal or near-black background.
- Subtle fade transition when a colour is selected.
- Preserve the original image aspect ratio.
- Display the full outfit without awkward cropping.

### Right section

- Product title: **FSD Modest Flow**.
- Subtitle: **Women's Modest Performance Activewear Set**.
- Short approved product description.
- Selected colour name.
- Six colour-selection swatches.
- Approved product information, where available.
- A restrained close button.

Use subtle champagne-gold borders or highlights to indicate the selected colour.

Avoid oversized cards, excessive shadows, heavy animations or cluttered galleries.

The overlay must be easy to close and work correctly on desktop, tablet and mobile.

---

## 3. SIX APPROVED COLOUR EDITIONS

Create these exact selectable options:

1. **Onyx Black (Core)** — `#0D0D0D`
2. **Deep Olive (Earth)** — `#2B3227`
3. **Warm Taupe (Modest)** — `#8A7B70`
4. **Slate Grey (Tech)** — `#666B70`
5. **Midnight Navy (Performance)** — `#1B2432`
6. **Deep Burgundy (Combat)** — `#4A1521`

Each option must have:

- Its own colour swatch.
- Its exact colour name.
- Its corresponding approved individual product image.
- A clear selected state.

The selected colour name must update immediately when a swatch is clicked.

The swatches should be keyboard accessible and have descriptive accessibility labels.

**Use the six individual colour photographs already generated and approved for this product.**

Do not use the six-colour comparison board as the changing main product image.

---

## 4. CRITICAL IMAGE BEHAVIOUR

**SHOW ONLY ONE MAIN PRODUCT IMAGE AT A TIME.**

When a customer clicks a colour swatch:

1. Update the selected colour state.
2. Replace the currently displayed product image with the approved individual photograph for that colour.
3. Apply a smooth, subtle crossfade.
4. Update the selected colour label.
5. Highlight the active swatch.

**Do not:**

- Generate additional image galleries automatically.
- Split the six-colour comparison board into gallery entries.
- Display a carousel of multiple product images for each colour.
- Stack all six product photographs on the screen.
- Use artificial CSS recolouring of the garments.
- Change the model's pose or gym background when switching between the six approved images.
- Alter the approved outfit, fabric appearance, Serene Monogram, hijab or garment construction.
- Replace the fixed campaign hero when a colour changes.

The six approved colour photographs already exist and should be treated as **six individual image assets, one per colour**.

If additional front, back, side or detail images are required later, prepare those separately for approval. Do not generate them as part of this implementation.

## 6. VISUAL INTERACTION DESIGN

The interaction should feel luxurious and effortless.

### Opening the product panel

- Soft fade and restrained slide.
- No page jump.
- No automatic scrolling.
- Background page remains in position.

### Selecting a colour

- Immediate responsive feedback.
- Gentle image crossfade.
- Champagne-gold active indicator.
- No distracting zoom or spin animations.

### Closing the panel

- Smooth fade out.
- Restore focus to the product that opened it.
- Keep the underlying page position unchanged.

Support Escape-key closing, accessible focus management and reduced-motion preferences.

The Serene Monogram should remain a **small, flat matte champagne-gold garment emblem**, not a large chrome-style logo.
