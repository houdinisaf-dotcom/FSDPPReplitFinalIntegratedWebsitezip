## SECTION 4 — WOMEN'S MODEST ACTIVEWEAR REQUIREMENTS

Give particular attention to Muslim women and other customers seeking comfortable, practical modest sportswear.

Clearly distinguish:

- Loose-fitting modest garments
- Technical close-fitting performance garments
- Compression rash guards
- Leggings intended for layering
- Extended-coverage sports hijabs
- Everyday sports hijabs
- Running-specific sports hijabs

Modest garments should be described according to their actual intended coverage, including the chest, waist, hips, seat, arms and legs.

Do not market close-fitting leggings or compression tops as standalone modest outerwear.

Explain which products can be paired together to create complete modest training outfits.
