# FSD website content & design brief
## Objectives
Prepare a premium FSD Fitness & Self Defence website foundation without launching an active store. Showcase brand identity and planned categories, allow future product images and prices to be added later, and support eventual conversion when products are verified.

## Audience
Fitness, training, self-defence and active-lifestyle audiences; including performance apparel and modest activewear shoppers.

## Core navigation
Home | About FSD | Collections | Future Innovations | Journal | Contact
Collections may contain Men's Training Wear, Women's Training Wear, Modest Activewear & Sports Hijabs, Combat & MMA, Training Sets, Accessories, Packaging / Gift Sets. Verify product inclusion against the original register.

## Design system
Background #080808; gold accent #D9AE60; champagne #F2D49A; off-white #F5F0E8. Large editorial headings, understated thin gold dividers, mobile-first navigation, strong contrast and accessible text. Avoid putting lengthy copy directly into raster images.

## SEO & performance
Unique titles and meta descriptions for real pages; no-index draft staging environment where appropriate; descriptive alt text based on final images; compressed WebP/AVIF variants; lazy loading; structured Product data ONLY when validated and actually applicable; analytics only with appropriate consent. Internal links between collections, product placeholders, About and Contact.

## Safety of claims
Avoid definitive claims about medical effectiveness, sensor accuracy, waterproof ratings, GPS, battery life, health monitoring, app compatibility or certifications until confirmed.
